# Annotations

> How can this be? This is a nearly 10 year friendship, and you don’t know something that intimate about me? My favourite music? Songs I like? This revelation almost made me spiral.

> Sometimes there’s something lonelier than being alone. It’s being surrounded by people who only know their version of you. Not the true version.

> What I think hurt wasn’t that she didn’t know I liked Nirvana. It was what that moment symbolised. The slow realisation that there are entire rooms inside of me that some of my friends have never walked into. Not because they’re locked. But because no one’s ever knocked.

> Talking is easy. Talking is updates, recaps, headlines. Discussing is intimate. It requires curiosity. It means staying with a thought long enough to pull it apart. It means asking why someone loves something, not just nodding and moving on.

> With some of my friends, I realised we don’t build memories, we exchange information. I know what’s happening to them, but not what’s happening inside them, and they don’t know that about me either.
